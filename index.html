How to play

  • 1Drag your finger from a house to a building of the same colour to lay a road.
  • 2Buildings show white dots when they need visitors. Cars leave home to fill them.
  • 3If a building has too many dots for too long, a timer fills. When it's full, the game ends.
  • 4Road tiles run out. Each week you get more, plus an upgrade: bridges cross water, roundabouts ease busy junctions, motorways jump straight between two points.
  • 5Pause any time. You can still draw while paused. Pinch to zoom.
